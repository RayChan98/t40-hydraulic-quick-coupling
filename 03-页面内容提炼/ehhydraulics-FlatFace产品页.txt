Are flat face connectors compatible with my equipment?
Our flat face connectors comply with ISO 16028 standards, ensuring compatibility with a wide range of hydraulic equipment, including skid steers, excavators, and tractors. Available in sizes including 1/4-inch, 3/8-inch, 1/2-inch, and 3/4-inch, and with NPT or SAE threads, they can be used as direct OEM replacements, eliminating integration challenges with existing systems.
Can flat face connectors be used in high-pressure hydraulic systems?
Yes, our flat face connectors are designed for high-pressure applications and are rated for pressures up to 5,000 PSI. Their rugged construction ensures reliable performance in heavy equipment, alleviating concerns about leaks or failures under extreme conditions.
How do flat face connectors prevent contamination in hydraulic systems?
The flush design of the flat face connectors allows users to wipe the surface before connection, minimizing the risk of dirt, dust, or debris entering the hydraulic circuit. An optional dust cap further protects the connector when not in use, extending the life of your hydraulic system.
Are your flat face connectors easy to connect and disconnect?
Yes, our flat face connectors feature a push-on connection design that allows one-handed operation, making attachment changes quick and easy. This user-friendly feature saves time on the jobsite and improves operational efficiency.
What sizes and thread types are available for flat face connectors?
We offer a variety of flat face connector sizes, including 1/4", 3/8", 1/2", 3/4", and 1", with thread options such as NPT and SAE. We also offer custom configurations to meet specific equipment needs, ensuring customers find the right hydraulic quick connector for their application.
